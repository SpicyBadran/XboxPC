Prior investigation was **directionally correct** (OSU1 is update payload, not full OS; public GDK is not retail OS; no public PC boot path) but **over-claimed certainty** on platform-level blockers. Ten claims presented as *Confirmed / Strongly supported* lacked direct evidence binding the retail Xbox Series X *operating system* to a specific hardware check. Corrections below downgrade those to **INFERRED** or **UNKNOWN** and retain the overall verdict with honest uncertainty.

**Counts before correction:** Confirmed 3–4, Strongly supported 7, Inferred few.  
**Counts after correction:** `VERIFIED` 6, `STRONGLY SUPPORTED` 7, `INFERRED` 8, `UNKNOWN` 5, `CONTRADICTED` 0.

No claim was fabricated; all had at least plausible basis. Correction is about calibration, not reversal.

## 4. What Is VERIFIED After Audit (High-Confidence Facts Another Engineer Can Independently Reproduce)

- **V1.** Offline update distribution exists: Xbox Support page, Windows PC + 6 GB NTFS USB, filename `OSU1.zip` → `$SystemUpdate/` (Support page shell + 5+ corroborations) — reproducible via `fetch_page` + Win PC download.
- **V2.** OSU1 is **update, not full OS nor disk image**: consumed from within Xbox Startup Troubleshooter onto pre-existing `System Update` partitions (Support page + greyed-out-version + GBAtemp A/B slots) — reproducible logic.
- **V3.** Public GDK is game SDK, not retail OS; Secure Downloads are **GDK + GDKX recoveries, offline docs, Submission Validator** — personal MSAs excluded, Secure Downloads requires MS rep — directly quoted from `access-resources` page.
- **V4.** Xbox Series X SoC is custom Scarlett: TSMC N7E 360.4 mm², **15.3B transistors** (Hot Chips 2020 slide 3), **16 GB GDDR6 10+6 interleaves, 20 ch x16 @14 Gbps → 560 GB/s, 320 banks** (slide 5), **8× Zen 2 @3.8 GHz (3.6 w/SMT) + Xbox SPLEAP anti-EoP** (slide 5), **GPU 1.825 GHz 52 CUs 12 TFLOPS 3328 SP + SFS/DXR/VRS/Mesh** (slide 5), **HSP/Pluton Root of Trust + SHACK + MSP crypto/hash/decomp @ SSD BW** — all **verified** from Hot Chips pdf slides `HC32_HotChips2020_GPU_Microsoft_Jeff_Andrews_v2.pdf` + AnandTech live-blog 09:07PM–09:15PM.
- **V5.** Bootloaders follow fused-ROM → SP chain generally (Xbox One diagram + Hot Chips “HSP/Pluton RoT”); Hydra hypervisor runs HostOS/SystemOS/GameOS via VBIs/host.xvd/system.xvd/ERA VBI — strongly supported from wiki 2024-2025 + 2026 Homebrew retrospective.
- **V6.** Generic modern PCs present **different** CPU family/model (Wikichip 840F70 Cardinal/880F40 Project X vs Matisse/Rome), **different memory pools** (DIMM split vs unified GDDR6), **different storage topology** (standard NVMe without Xbox GPT GUIDs or MSP offload semantics), and **different driver/GPU firmware coupling** — sufficient to defeat drop-in boot, even without invoking specific allowlist string.
